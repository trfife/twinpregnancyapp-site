What it is

Most pregnancy apps assume one baby. Twin pregnancies move on a different cadence, with more scans, more planning, and twice the milestones. This app leans all the way into the twins niche.

  • Twins-only focus
  • Week-by-week (weeks 7–40)
  • Local-first & offline
  • Free
  • No ads, no tracking

Five tabs, built for two babies

Everything is per-twin: separate sizes, separate weights, twin-specific cadence.

H

Home

Greeting, current week, combined twin size, per-baby weight, and a due-date countdown at a glance.

W

Week

Week-by-week guide: "For mom," "Your twins," and "To-do," with sizes, weights, and easy prev/next.

P

Plan

Per-week to-do checklist plus curated twin gear suggestions to keep double planning simple.

T

Track

Weight log and appointment list. Twin pregnancies mean many more scans to stay on top of.

M

More

Curated news, settings, themes, reminders, and one-tap delete-all-data. Your data stays yours.
