Building a registry for twins isn't just a singleton list times two. Some things you genuinely need in pairs, some you can share, and a few twin-specific items make the early months far easier. Here's how to split it so you don't overbuy or come up short.

What to buy two of

A handful of items truly need a one-per-baby count. These are the doubles worth registering for first.

Two car seats

Each baby needs their own rear-facing car seat from the first ride home, so put two infant car seats near the top of the list.

Two safe sleep spaces

Twins should each have their own sleep surface. Two bassinets keep both close in the early weeks without sharing a space.

What you can share

Plenty of big-ticket items work fine as a single shared purchase, which keeps the registry total reasonable.

One double stroller

A single twin stroller covers both babies. Decide on side-by-side versus tandem before you register.

One changing station

A twin nursing pillow

A double electric pump

Stock up in bulk

Two babies burn through consumables fast. Register for larger counts than a singleton list suggests.

  • Newborn and size 1 diapers—sizes go by weight, so check the packs
  • Two sets of swaddles, plenty of burp cloths, and extra crib sheets
  • A double helping of bottles if you plan to bottle or combo feed
