Twins arrive earlier on average than singletons, so pack your bag by around 30 weeks. Plan for a possible NICU stay, double the newborn essentials, and a slightly longer hospital stay than you'd expect with one baby.

For mom

  • ID, insurance card, and your birth plan
  • Long phone charger and a comfy going-home outfit
  • Toiletries and lip balm; hospital air is dry
  • A nursing pillow sized for tandem feeding two babies

For the babies (×2)

  • Two going-home outfits in newborn and preemie sizes
  • Two swaddle blankets and two hats
  • Two installed infant car seats—done before discharge

If the NICU is likely

Preterm twins may spend time in the NICU. Pack a few extra preemie items, a notebook for tracking feeds, and comfortable layers for long days at the bedside.
