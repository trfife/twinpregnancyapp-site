A double stroller is the one big-ticket item most twin parents use every single day. The right one fits your doorways, your car trunk, and your two babies for years. The wrong one lives in the garage. Here is how to choose, plus picks for every budget.

Three layouts, three trade-offs

Twin strollers come in three shapes. Pick the layout first; the brand matters less than the fit.

  • Side-by-side: equal seats, easy to recline both, great for trails and parks. Wider, so measure your doorways.
  • Tandem (front-and-back): narrower for shops and elevators, but the back seat can feel cramped as toddlers grow.
  • Snap-and-go frame: a lightweight frame that holds two infant car seats. Cheapest, lightest, perfect for the newborn months before you commit.

What actually matters for twins

  • Both seats recline flat for newborns and nap on the move.
  • Folded width fits your trunk, so measure before you buy.
  • One-hand fold, because you'll always be holding a baby.
  • Car-seat compatibility with two infant seats (check the brand's adapter list).
