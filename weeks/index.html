Twin pregnancy, week by week

A quick look at how your twins grow each week — their size and a highlight — from week 7 to your due date. Open any week in the free app for the full per-twin guide.

Get the full guide in the app

Every week, two babies

Each week below shows what your twins are sized like plus one quick highlight. The full app adds per-twin sizes and weights, "for mom," "your twins," to-dos, and cited sources.

7

Week 7 · First trimester

Each twin is about as long as a grain of rice

Less than an ounce each. Tiny dimples mark where noses and ears will be, and little limb buds are starting to form the cartilage that will become arm and leg bones.

8

Week 8 · First trimester

Each twin is about the size of a blueberry

About 0.035 oz each. Arms are growing faster than legs, because the upper body develops first.

9

Week 9 · First trimester

Each twin is about the size of a grape

About 0.11 oz each. Their faces are becoming more recognizable, with eyelids, a little mouth, and a tongue with tiny taste buds.

10

Week 10 · First trimester

Each twin is about as long as a kumquat

About 0.18 oz each. Their faces are coming into proportion.

11

Week 11 · First trimester

Each twin is about as long as a fig

About 0.44 oz each. Their heads are still large compared with their bodies, but the bodies are catching up fast.

12

Week 12 · First trimester

Each twin is about as long as a lime

About 0.7 oz each. Their internal organs and muscles have grown, and their skeletons are slowly hardening from soft tissue into bone.

13

Week 13 · First trimester

Each twin is about as long as a pea pod

About 1.4 oz each. Their ovaries or testes are fully developed inside, with final changes still happening on the outside.

14

Week 14 · Second trimester

Each twin is about as long as a lemon

About 2.1 oz each. Their heads are rounder and more in proportion with their bodies.

15

Week 15 · Second trimester

Each twin is about as long as an apple

About 3.9 oz each (typical for twins with two placentas). They're growing a soft, fine hair called lanugo all over their bodies, and their eyebrows and eyelashes are starting to develop.

16

Week 16 · Second trimester

Each twin is about the size of an avocado

About 5.0 oz each (typical for twins with two placentas). They're starting to pull faces, though any smile or frown is random for now, since they don't yet have control of those muscles.

17

Week 17 · Second trimester

Each twin is about the size of a turnip

About 6.2 oz each (typical for twins with two placentas). They can move their eyes, though the lids stay shut, open and close their mouths, and react to loud noises.

18

Week 18 · Second trimester

Each twin is about the size of a bell pepper

About 7.7 oz each (typical for twins with two placentas). Their hearing, swallowing and sucking reflexes are all developing, and they're wriggling and stretching their arms and legs a lot.

19

Week 19 · Second trimester

Each twin weighs about as much as a tomato

About 9.5 oz each (typical for twins with two placentas). They're starting to look a little plumper and less wrinkly as thin layers of fat form under their skin.

20

Week 20 · Second trimester

Each twin is about as long as a banana

About 11.6 oz each (typical for twins with two placentas). Babies are now measured from head to heel instead of head to bottom, which is why the length jumps this week.

21

Week 21 · Second trimester

Each twin is about as long as a carrot

About 13.9 oz each (typical for twins with two placentas). Each baby is now heavier than their placenta, which keeps growing to do its job of feeding them and clearing waste.

22

Week 22 · Second trimester

Each twin is about as long as a spaghetti squash

About 17 oz (1.06 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). Their lungs aren't ready for life outside the womb yet, but they're developing fast, and each baby is practicing breathing movements with their diaphragm.

23

Week 23 · Second trimester

Each twin weighs about as much as a mango

About 20 oz (1.25 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). Their arms and legs are now in proportion with the rest of their bodies.

24

Week 24 · Second trimester

Each twin is about as long as an ear of corn

About 23 oz (1.44 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). Their bodies are well proportioned but still small, with lots of growing ahead.

25

Week 25 · Second trimester

Each twin weighs about as much as a rutabaga

About 27 oz (1.69 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). It's an active time: a loud noise may make them jump, and you might feel the rhythmic little jolts of hiccups.

26

Week 26 · Second trimester

Each twin is about as long as a scallion

About 31 oz (1.94 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). Their eyes are opening for the first time, and soon they'll learn to blink.

27

Week 27 · Second trimester

Each twin weighs about as much as a head of cauliflower

About 35 oz (2.19 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). They're getting plumper by the day as fat fills out the folds in their skin.

28

Week 28 · Third trimester

Each twin weighs about as much as an eggplant

About 40 oz (2.50 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). Their heart rate has slowed to around 140 beats per minute, and it will be closer to 130 at birth.

29

Week 29 · Third trimester

Each twin is about the size of a butternut squash

About 46 oz (2.88 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). The coming weeks are about maturing their organs and gaining fat.

30

Week 30 · Third trimester

Each twin weighs about as much as a cabbage

About 51 oz (3.19 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). Their eyes can focus now, and they're practicing breathing movements more regularly.

31

Week 31 · Third trimester

Each twin weighs about as much as a coconut

About 57 oz (3.56 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). They're very active, moving around, sucking their fingers and turning over.

32

Week 32 · Third trimester

Each twin weighs about as much as a jicama

About 64 oz (4.00 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). The coming weeks are about gaining weight and fat, which helps them keep warm after birth.

33

Week 33 · Third trimester

Each twin is about the size of a pineapple

About 70 oz (4.38 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). After birth you'll feel soft spots, called fontanelles, on each baby's head where the skull bones haven't joined yet.

34

Week 34 · Third trimester

Each twin weighs about as much as a cantaloupe

About 77 oz (4.81 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). They're curled up with their legs bent toward their chests, and space is getting tight, but you should still feel them shifting and see your bump change shape.

35

Week 35 · Third trimester

Each twin weighs about as much as a honeydew melon

About 84 oz (5.25 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). They're getting chubbier, and that extra fat will help them stay warm once they're born.

36

Week 36 · Third trimester

Each twin is about as long as a head of romaine lettuce

About 91 oz (5.69 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). Your team will confirm how each baby is lying close to the birth, because it helps decide the safest way for them to be born.

37

Week 37 · Third trimester

Each twin is about as long as a Swiss chard leaf

About 98 oz (6.12 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). They're trying out facial expressions like frowns and smiles, though these are random for now.

38

Week 38 · Third trimester

Each twin is about as long as a leek

About 104 oz (6.50 lb) each (typical for twins with two placentas). Most of their lanugo hair has gone, though some babies are born with patches here and there.

39

Week 39 · Third trimester

This week's single-baby size comparison is a mini watermelon

Twin weight charts stop at 38 weeks, so there's no twin weight for this week. Their skin has grown a tougher outer layer that helps with temperature control, and it's still coated in waxy vernix.

40

Week 40 · Third trimester

This week's single-baby size comparison is a small pumpkin

Twin weight charts stop at 38 weeks, so there's no twin weight for this week. Your babies should keep moving in their usual pattern right up to labor. If movements change, call your maternity unit, even at night.

Sources: development notes from the NHS week-by-week guide and Tommy’s week-by-week pages. Weights for weeks 15 to 38 are the middle (50th percentile) estimate for twins with two placentas in Grantz et al. 2016 (NICHD), not a target; twins who share a placenta weren’t in that study. Lengths and earlier weights come from single-baby charts. Your scans are the check on your own babies’ growth.
