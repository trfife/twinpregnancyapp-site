Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 28, 2026

Twin Pregnancy App is built to be private by design. This policy explains, in plain language, what the app does and does not do with your information.

The short version

  • Everything stays on your device. Your due date, your twins' names and genders, your preferences, and anything else you enter are stored locally on your phone.
  • No accounts. There's nothing to sign up for and no login.
  • No servers. The app has no backend. Nothing you enter is uploaded, synced, or sent to us, because there's nowhere for it to go.
  • No tracking, no ads. We don't run analytics or advertising, and we don't build a profile of you.

What we store, and where

The information you provide (your due date, twin details, theme choice, reminder preference, and the medical-disclaimer acceptance) is saved in local storage on your device. It is used only to personalize what you see in the app. You can erase all of it at any time from More → Delete all my data.

Notifications

Weekly check-in reminders are optional. They are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device; turning them on does not send anything to us or to a third party. You can switch them off in More → Reminders at any time.

Amazon affiliate links

A few product suggestions link to Amazon. If you buy through one, the app may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you, as an Amazon Associate. These links open in your browser; we don't receive your name, your purchases, or any personal details, only Amazon's standard, anonymous referral credit.

No data sharing

Because data never leaves your device, we have nothing to sell, share, or hand over. There are no third-party data processors.

Children

This app is intended for expectant parents and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be included in the app and this document. The date at the top reflects the latest revision.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach out through the app's listing on your app store, or see our support page.
