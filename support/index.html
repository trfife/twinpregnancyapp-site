Frequently asked questions

Is the app really free?

Yes. The core experience is free with no ads. A few gear suggestions link to Amazon as affiliate links, which is the only way the app earns anything.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All weekly content and images are bundled with the app, so it works fully without a network connection.

Where is my data stored?

Entirely on your device. There are no accounts and no servers. You can delete everything at any time from More → Delete all my data. See our Privacy Policy for details.

Why a twins-only app?

Twin pregnancies follow a different cadence, with more scans and double planning. Everything here is per-twin, so you can follow both babies without adapting a single-baby app.

When will it be available?

Android first, coming to Google Play. iOS and an installable web app are planned to follow.

Privacy Policy Support

As an Amazon Associate, the app earns from qualifying purchases. Some in-app product suggestions link to Amazon and may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Twin Pregnancy App provides general information only and is not medical advice; talk to your provider.
